常見問題

GoClip 是什麼？

GoClip 讓你挑幾支影片或照片、選一首歌，就自動把畫面切在音樂節拍上，做出踩點短片。所有運算都在你的手機上完成，不需要網路，也不需要註冊帳號。

需要網路連線嗎？

不需要。GoClip 完全離線運作。

我的影片會被上傳嗎？

不會。App 沒有任何伺服器，你的素材與成品只存在你的手機裡。詳見隱私權政策。

可以用自己的音樂嗎？

可以，你能從「檔案」App 匯入自己的音訊檔。GoClip 也內建一套 CC0 / CC BY 授權的免費曲庫。

輸出的影片規格是什麼？

1080×1920（直式 9:16），H.264 MP4，儲存到你的相簿。

成品可以商用嗎？

你自己拍攝的素材權利屬於你。若使用了內建曲庫中的 CC BY 授權曲目，公開發布時請附上 App 內顯示的作者署名。CC0 曲目則無此限制。

App 閃退或輸出失敗

請試著：重新啟動 App、確認裝置剩餘空間充足（輸出 1080p 影片需要數百 MB）、避免一次匯入超過 100 個素材。若問題持續，來信告訴我們你的 iPhone 型號、iOS 版本與發生當下的操作步驟。

回報問題

寄到 beemen.official@gmail.com，請附上：iPhone 型號、iOS 版本、GoClip 版本，以及問題的重現步驟。我們通常在兩個工作天內回覆。

FAQ

What is GoClip?

Pick a few videos or photos, choose a song, and GoClip automatically cuts your footage to the beat. Everything is processed on your device — no internet, no account required.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. GoClip works fully offline.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. There are no servers. Your media and exports stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use my own music?

Yes — import your own audio from the Files app. GoClip also ships with a built-in library of CC0 / CC BY licensed tracks.

What is the export format?

1080×1920 (vertical 9:16), H.264 MP4, saved to your photo library.

Can I use my exports commercially?

You own the rights to footage you shot. If you used a CC BY track from the built-in library, include the attribution shown in the app when publishing. CC0 tracks carry no such requirement.

The app crashes or export fails

Try restarting the app, check that you have enough free storage (a 1080p export needs several hundred MB), and avoid importing more than 100 clips at once. If it persists, email us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing.

Report a Problem

Email beemen.official@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, GoClip version, and steps to reproduce. We usually reply within two business days.
